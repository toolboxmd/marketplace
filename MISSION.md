discoverable, understandable, autonomously installable under granted authority,
loadable, and outcome-ready across supported agentic harnesses. It ingests
immutable released Project Records, adds outcome-oriented Agent Search

machine-readable discovery, and proves published Agent Modules through real
installation and use.
