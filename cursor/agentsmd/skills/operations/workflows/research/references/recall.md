# Recall prior work

Distinguish resuming one task from recalling work across tasks or learning
personal preferences. Preference capture belongs to [reflection](../../reflection/index.md). One task begins with its canonical Issue handoff and live
state under `operations`; do not mine transcripts when that record is sufficient.
For cross-task recall, establish topic, workspace, and time range. Preserve an
explicit request for all history rather than silently reducing it to a week.

Start with supplied references and the owning tracker. Use host task/history
APIs when sufficient. Transcript filesystem layouts are host-specific adapters,
not portable assumptions. Select relevant records using real timestamps and
content, not UUID ordering. Exclude unrelated projects and conversations outside
the authorized scope.
Retrieve the pertinent interval before loading a whole transcript.

Summaries can locate work. A disputed claim about what ran, what failed, or what
was decided needs the relevant original record. Search previous attempts,
recurring reports, reverts, and unresolved errors when they could change the
resume point. Preserve explicit user corrections and rejected approaches with
their reason; a polished final summary may omit the failure that matters now.

Reconcile mutable claims against Git, current Issues/PRs, artifacts, applicable
instructions, and relevant live state. A transcript proves a statement or past
action, not current delivery. Reuse valid proof and completed decisions; changed
candidate identity, inputs, environment, or material claims require the applicable
revalidation. Do not restart completed work from a stale transcript.

Return a compact capsule covering every in-scope task: wanted outcome, truthful
current lifecycle state, important decisions, failed approaches, blockers, exact
evidence, and next useful action. Allow unknown and mixed states such as released
but not installed. Do not force a falsely complete status into a small vocabulary.
Keep private provenance local and sanitize before any authorized public use.
